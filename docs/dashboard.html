About this data

Source: Case 31, an employee credit union (EFCU) member survey provided for this course, with 128 respondents.

Scales: Employee and credit union items use 1 = Strongly Disagree to 5 = Strongly Agree. The employee score is the average of the four employee items.

Left out on purpose: Overall ratings of “No Opinion” and blank answers are excluded from the overall rating chart. The product awareness questions (Q13 to Q26) contain a third answer code that has no label in the data file, so they are not charted until that code can be confirmed.

Why this data: My CEP client data from Cal Poly Pomona’s Collins College has not arrived yet, so this dashboard uses the course case data to show the same kind of survey reporting I will build for the CEP.
